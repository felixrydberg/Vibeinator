## Area

apps/server

## Problem or use case

Keep complete supported history after operational consumers prune their delivery rows.

## Proposed solution

Add append/read APIs and chunked large payloads with stable IDs, integrity hashes and source links. Persist atomically under existing database ownership. Keep the current bounded runtime journal and its cursor/checkpoint protections. Backfill surviving existing content with explicit coverage metadata; do not claim previously pruned payloads were recovered.

## Why this matters

Keep complete project context available across restarts and provider handoffs while extending the retained Synara implementation.

## Smallest useful scope

Large Unicode text/JSON can be reconstructed byte-for-byte according to the encoding contract; duplicate accepted identities are idempotent; differing content under one identity is rejected; reads are paged; interrupted writes leave no published partial record. Operational pruning and database reopen do not remove archive content. Disk failure tests establish the contract used by 007.

## Alternatives considered

Reuse the existing Electron app and provider integrations; a framework migration or provider rewrite is outside this plan.

## Risks or tradeoffs

Priority: P0. Dependencies: 003. Estimate: 2–4 days.

Preserve existing conversation data, approval boundaries and provider lifecycle behavior. Additive schema work must follow the migration contract and include recovery evidence.
